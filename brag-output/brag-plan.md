# Brag Plan: JanSahayak

## What is this app?
JanSahayak is India's next-generation AI municipal intelligence and grievance redressal platform, connecting colloquial citizen voice reports directly to cross-department field remediation and closed-loop citizen verification in seconds.

## The angle
In municipal administration, one subterranean breakdown causes four different surface emergencies across four uncoordinated departments. A pipeline rupture under the road causes water contamination (DJB), a road cavity (PWD), garbage overflow (MCD), and a live wire hazard (BSES). Citizens file four separate complaints and get trapped in bureaucratic dead-ends. JanSahayak uses AI to link these complaints into a single root-cause incident in 3 seconds, automates officer dispatch, and guarantees resolution with before-and-after photo sign-offs.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"1 broken underground pipe. 4 government departments. 0 accountability."
Slam into a split-screen visualization of 4 isolated bureaucratic silos with spinning status tickers, instantly shattered by the JanSahayak Civic Intelligence pulse.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Multilingual Colloquial Audio Triage**: A citizen speaks in natural Hinglish (*"Bhai Sector 14 mein paani ganda aa raha hai aur sadak dhas gayi"*). The AI Grievance DNA™ instantly transcribes, classifies the hazard, and assigns a strict 24h SLA.
- **Cross-Department Spatial Clustering & Satellite Map**: The map zooms into Rohini Sector 14. Real-time GIS pins from DJB (Water) and PWD (Roads) pulse and snap together into a single connected incident with root-cause telemetry.
- **Autonomous Officer Dispatch & Closed-Loop Sign-off**: Field Engineer Er. Sanjay Sharma receives a structured mobile SOP checklist; upon physical fix, the citizen receives an instant prompt to photograph and verify the repair before the ticket can close.

## Outro / punchline
"Civic governance without the runaround. JanSahayak — Aapki Awaaz, Ab Samjhi Jayegi."
Official Government of NCT Delhi seal with glowing national civic intelligence badge.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Citizen speaks a natural voice grievance via audio or drops a map pin.
2. **AI Analysis**: System generates Grievance DNA™, category, confidence score (98.4%), and 24h SLA.
3. **Clustering**: Multi-department linkage engine correlates 17 local complaints to 1 root cause.
4. **Resolution**: Officer completes work order, citizen takes completion photo, loop closes.
